The `vizij-face` profile declares the gaze target, `standard/vizij/gaze/target/x` and `…/y`: a point on the face's frame — origin top-left, `x` to the right, `y` downward, each in `[0, 1]` — resting at the centre, (0.5, 0.5), in the gaze tier. A face whose graphs read it lists it in `listKeys` as an `f32` in `[0, 1]` resting at 0.5. The profile has 93 keys.
