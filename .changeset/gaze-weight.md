The `vizij-face` profile declares the gaze target's weight, `standard/vizij/gaze/target/weight`: how strongly the eyes hold the target, an `f32` in `[0, 1]` resting at 0, in the gaze tier. At 0 the eyes do what the face itself does, whatever the target says; at 1 they hold the target. The profile has 94 keys.
