The package ships Arora's animation module, `@arora/animation` 3.0.0 (`arora-animation-module` 1), in place of Vizij's `@vizij/animation` 2.0.0: its module directory, as a device loads the guest from it. `artifact/` holds `header.json`, the guest as `animation.wasm` (`wasmUrl` names it) and `records/`, the module record and the records of the structures its functions name, in the `arora` scope; `loadAnimationModule()` returns them as before. The module keeps its id and every function and parameter id, and adds `load_clip`, `reload_clip` (clip format 2.0), `set_rate`, `step_with_velocities`, `set_length` and `set_reversed`. It plays numbers as `f64`, keeps its players on the device time `step` is given, applies a command at the device time of the last step, leaves a player's playhead where it stands when its window changes, carries travel past a bound, and takes `bounce` as a loop mode. Its records keep the ids of `@vizij/animation`'s, each at the next minor: `PlayerState` 1.3.0 adds `reversed` and `waiting`, `InstanceState` 1.2.0 adds `rate`, and the others carry the same fields (`AnimationClip`, `AnimTrack` and `Keypoint` 1.2.0; `TransitionHandle`, `TrackOutput`, `StepValues`, `OutputKeys`, `BakedAnimation` and `BakedTrack` 1.1.0), so the records of both modules load into one registry.
