"@vizij/runtime": minor
---

A Vizij's animations load into its device's animation module with the face (unless `loadVizij(id, glb, { animations: false })`), each track writing the rig input its channel names, and `Runtime` loads, plays and unloads them through the module's declared functions, as any client does. `animations()` lists what the module holds, whoever loaded it (`{ id, duration }`, read from the player states at `vizij/animations/players`, each player named after its animation's id); `loadAnimation(animation)` loads one in `describe()`'s `animations` shape and resolves to `{ id, name, duration }` — loading an id already loaded replaces it, keeping the playback the module reports for it (playhead, loop mode, speed, weight, playing, paused or stopped); `unloadAnimation(id)` unloads one; `playAnimation(id, { reset, speed })`, `pauseAnimation`, `stopAnimation(id, { clearOutputs })`, `seekAnimation(id, seconds)`, `setAnimationLoop` and `setAnimationSpeed` drive its player, and reject for an id the device does not hold; `animationState(id)` reads `{ time, duration, playing, loop, speed, completed }` from the module. An animation loads silent, stopped at its start, looping at speed 1. `LoadedAnimation`, `AnimationState`, `PlayAnimationOptions` and `StopAnimationOptions` are exported types.
