A device's operations settle when nothing steps it. `loadGraph`, `applyGraphEdits`, `call`, `invoke`, `spawn` and `halt` resolve on a device with no `run()` going and no `step` from the page. Each is queued before it returns, and a `step(0)` in the next microtask applies it, so `await runtime.loadGraph(spec)` on a `startRuntime()` device resolves with the new graph already run once. That step is shared with the `setValue` and `writeValues` of the same turn, and applies everything in the order it was issued. A `step(0)` moves nothing that time drives: springs, dampers, slews, animations and program clocks hold. A `say` run follows its real-time audio. A page that calls `step` in the same turn applies the operations with its own step instead. Under `run()`, the run's next step applies them, and the page adds no step. `listKeys` and `describeMethods` answer at once, with no step, and do not see an operation still queued. A call issued from a callback the device makes during a step, such as the `audio` hook's playhead, is an ordinary call: a step cannot nest one, so the next step applies it, a `step(0)` once the current step has ended or, under `run()`, the run's next step.
